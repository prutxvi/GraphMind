## Data

Filing documents are public documents published by their issuers and by SEBI, NSE and BSE.
Footnote reads them on request; no filing PDF is redistributed in this repository, and no
filing content is stored in it. PAN and Aadhaar numbers are removed from stored text.

The UI icons are inline SVG drawn for this project. No third-party icon set is bundled.
